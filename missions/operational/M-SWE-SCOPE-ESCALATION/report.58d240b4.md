## What was found along the way

All fixed; the final SPEC.md and `core-rules.toml` additions carry no new deterministic findings.
- **A linter limitation surfaced while drafting COLOPHON.md**: the deterministic checker processes prose one physical source line at a time, so a backtick-wrapped phrase that straddles a markdown soft line-wrap is misparsed (the code-span boundary is lost), exposing the quoted word as live text and firing `banned-word` on words I had deliberately quoted as examples (`this is not only`, `it goes further than`, "not just" inside "do not just work around"). Worked around by following the existing house convention of one single joined code span for a list of fixed phrases (as SPEC.md's own "Deterministic-tier fixed list" paragraphs already do), rather than individually-backticked phrases that can wrap. Also found and fixed one live (non-quoted) use of the word "carry" as a verb, and one anthropomorphism finding ("moves") in that same drafting. Not filed as a separate issue against the linter itself — the workaround is a matter of how the prose is laid out, not a correctness gap in the checked content.
- **SPEC.md carries 23 pre-existing deterministic findings** (mostly `banned-word` on "the reader" and "holds", some `anthropomorphism`), all outside the lines this change touched, confirmed by line-number comparison before and after. Not fixed, as cleaning up a 2,500-line pre-existing document is unrelated, much larger work outside this issue's scope.
